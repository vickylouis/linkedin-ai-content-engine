# Privacy Policy – LinkedIn AI Content Engine

**Last Updated:** November 4, 2026

## 1. Introduction

LinkedIn AI Content Engine is a personal automation project designed to generate, review, manage, and publish LinkedIn content.

This Privacy Policy explains how information is processed when using this application.

## 2. Information We Process

The application may process the following information:

- LinkedIn post content, hashtags, and publishing status.
- LinkedIn account authorization information.
- Content and approval records stored in Google Sheets.
- Technical information required to operate the automation workflow.

## 3. How Information Is Used

Information is used only to:

- Generate and review LinkedIn content.
- Manage content approval and publishing workflows.
- Publish posts to the authorized LinkedIn account.
- Maintain publishing records and prevent duplicate posts.

## 4. Third-Party Services

The application may use the following services:

- **LinkedIn:** For account authorization and publishing approved posts.
- **Google Sheets:** For storing and managing content records.
- **n8n:** For workflow automation.
- **AI service providers:** For generating and reviewing content.

These services process information according to their own privacy policies and terms.

## 5. Data Storage and Security

Application data may be stored in Google Sheets and the local n8n environment.

OAuth credentials and access tokens are intended to be stored in n8n's credential management system and should not be shared publicly.

Reasonable measures are taken to protect application data and access credentials.

## 6. Data Sharing

Personal information is not sold or rented to third parties.

Information is shared with the third-party services described above only as necessary to operate the application.

## 7. Data Retention and Deletion

Content records may be retained in Google Sheets for workflow management and publishing history.

The application operator can delete stored content records and revoke LinkedIn authorization when no longer required.

## 8. User Control

The LinkedIn account owner controls authorization and can revoke the application's access through LinkedIn account settings.

## 9. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when application functionality or data processing practices change.
